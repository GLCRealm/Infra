/05 · Questions

BEFORE YOU
GREENLIGHT US.

Answers to what founders usually ask before starting a web project.

Do we get the source code, not just a hosted link? +
Yes — every project ships with the full source repository handed over, so your team owns the codebase fully and isn't locked into us to make changes.
What happens after the site launches? +
Launch Build and Full Platform include a short post-launch warranty window. For ongoing monitoring, patches, and content updates, the Maintenance Retainer keeps a team on call.
Can you work with our existing site instead of rebuilding it? +
Yes — a lot of our work is auditing and improving an existing site rather than starting from scratch. We'll flag what's worth keeping before proposing a rebuild.
What stack do you build on? +
We choose the stack based on the project's needs and your team's ability to maintain it afterward, rather than defaulting to whatever we used last time.
What's the typical turnaround for a full platform build? +
Six to ten weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope and how quickly feedback rounds come back to us.
