/05 · Questions

IS THIS
FOR YOU?

Answers to what founders usually ask before booking a consultation.

Do I need a polished brief before we talk? +
No — bring the numbers, the doubts, and the competing priorities. The consultation exists to create structure from that mess, not to review a finished deck.
What do I actually leave with? +
Depending on the engagement: a structured business diagnosis, a priority matrix, a growth roadmap, and a 30/60/90-day action plan — not just notes from a conversation.
Is this only for early-stage founders? +
No — we work with founders who are growing but unclear on the next move, as well as established teams whose priorities keep changing under pressure.
Can you help with one specific decision instead of a full engagement? +
Yes — the Strategy Session is built for exactly that: a single working session around one pricing, positioning, or go/no-go decision.
What's the typical turnaround for a growth sprint? +
Two to three weeks from kickoff to a delivered roadmap, depending on scope and how quickly follow-up sessions get scheduled.
