/05 · Questions

BEFORE YOU
BRIEF US.

Answers to what founders usually ask before starting a creative project.

Do we get the source files, not just exports? +
Yes — every project ships with editable source files (Figma, Adobe, or the native format of the work) plus export packages, so your team owns the work fully and can extend it later.
How many revision rounds are included? +
Design Sprint includes two rounds. Full Identity and the Retainer include unlimited rounds within the agreed scope — we'd rather get it right than count revisions.
Do you only show one direction, not several? +
By default, yes — we present one strong, well-argued direction rather than three diluted options. If you'd prefer to see alternates explored, we can scope that in upfront.
Can you work from an existing brand instead of starting fresh? +
Yes — a lot of our work is extending or sharpening an existing identity rather than replacing it. We'll audit what's working before proposing changes.
What's the typical turnaround for a full identity? +
Three to five weeks from kickoff to final delivery, depending on scope and how quickly feedback rounds come back to us.
