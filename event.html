PLANNING THAT
SURVIVES CONTACT.

Every event ships with a full run-of-show, vendor contacts, and a post-event report — so what happened on the day is never a mystery to your team afterward.

/05 · Questions

BEFORE YOU
BOOK US.

Answers to what founders usually ask before planning an event.

Do you handle vendor contracts and payments directly? +
Yes — we negotiate and contract venues and vendors on your behalf, and manage payments against the budget agreed upfront, so you're not chasing five different invoices.
Will your team actually be on-site on the day? +
Always. Full Production and the Retainer include a dedicated on-site producer running the timeline live; Launch Day includes an execution team for the full event window.
Can you work with a venue or vendor we've already picked? +
Yes — a lot of our work is stepping into a partly-planned event rather than starting from zero. We'll audit what's booked before filling in the rest.
What happens if something goes wrong on the day? +
Every event ships with a contingency plan built in at the planning stage, and our on-site team is authorized to make real-time calls — you're never the first to hear about a problem.
What's the typical timeline for a full production? +
Six to ten weeks from kickoff to event day, depending on scale and how far out the venue and vendors need to be locked in.
