/01 · About us

WHO WE ARE.

InfraEdge 360 is a hybrid creative studio for founders who don't have time to manage six vendors. One team, one point of contact, six service lines — legal, consulting, creative, media, events, and web — run by senior operators, not middlemen.

Vision

To be the single infrastructure layer every ambitious brand plugs into, replacing a maze of freelancers and agencies with one accountable partner.

Mission

Deliver senior-level execution across every discipline, at startup speed, without the agency markup or the freelancer chaos.
