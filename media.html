/05 · Questions

BEFORE YOU
BOOK A SHOOT.

Answers to what founders usually ask before starting a media project.

Do we get the raw footage, not just the final cut? +
Yes — every shoot ships with the full raw library alongside the finished cuts, so your team owns the footage fully and can re-edit or repurpose it later.
How many revision rounds are included? +
Content Sprint includes one round. Full Campaign and the Retainer include unlimited rounds within the agreed scope — we'd rather get the cut right than count revisions.
Can you deliver formats for multiple platforms from one shoot? +
Yes — we plan coverage upfront so one shoot day can yield vertical, square, and landscape cuts without reshooting, as long as it's scoped in the brief.
Can you edit footage we already shot ourselves? +
Yes — send us the raws and we can handle color, sound, and the edit pass on its own, no reshoot required.
What's the typical turnaround for a full campaign? +
Three to five weeks from kickoff to final delivery, depending on scope and how quickly feedback rounds come back to us.
