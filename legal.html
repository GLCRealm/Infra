/05 · Questions

BEFORE YOU
SIGN.

Answers to what founders usually ask before starting a legal engagement.

How fast can you incorporate a company? +
Name approval and filing typically take 7–10 working days once we have your documents, depending on ROC processing times. We track the filing until the certificate is issued and flag any delays as soon as we see them.
Can you review a contract we already have in place? +
Yes — send it over and we'll mark up risk clauses, missing protections, and anything unusually one-sided, with plain-language notes on what each flag means for you.
Do you handle trademark objections and renewals? +
Yes, end to end — search, class filing, objection responses if the registrar raises any, and renewal reminders so you don't lose a mark to a missed deadline.
Is the retainer a fixed monthly rate? +
Yes. One flat monthly fee covers drafting, review, and standing compliance work within scope — no per-question invoices. Matters outside scope (litigation, for example) are quoted separately before we start.
Do you work with teams outside Uttarakhand? +
Yes — most of our legal work happens over calls and shared documents, so location isn't a constraint. Filings are handled for entities anywhere in India.
